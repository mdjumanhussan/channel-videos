LONG VIDEO (post 18:00 Sydney, Sunday 8 Nov 2026)
Title: The Hidden Coil Inside Your Bank Card

Description:
Your bank card has no battery, yet it pays with a tap. This short animated explainer shows the hidden coil of wire inside the card, how the card reader powers it, and how the chip answers back.

What is covered:
- The flat coil of wire that runs around the edge of the card
- How the reader's coil makes a magnetic field that flips about 13.5 million times a second
- How that field powers the chip, the same idea as a wireless phone charger
- Quick question: how does the card answer without its own power?
- Load switching: how the reader feels ones and zeros
- Why you have to hold the card close
- The one-time code the chip makes for every payment
- Bus and train cards, passports with a chip, and phones that pay

The drawings are simplified for teaching. The amount, counter and code shown on screen are illustrative examples.
