Title: Why the Sea Is Salty but Rivers Aren't

Description:
Rain is fresh and rivers taste fresh, yet the sea is salty. This short animated explainer follows the trip from a mountain to the coast, and shows why salts pile up in the sea but pass through a river.

What is covered:
- How much salt is in seawater and in river water
- Why rain is a very weak acid, and what it does to rock
- How dissolved minerals travel down rivers: about 4 billion tons a year
- Quick question: does the salt rise when the sun lifts water off the sea?
- Why a river is a pipe and the sea is the end of the line
- Why river salts and sea salts are different
- Undersea vents and volcanoes
- Why the sea is not getting saltier, and how much salt it holds

The drawings are simplified for teaching and are not to scale. Figures are approximate and come from the sources below.
