Title: Why AI Chatbots Make Things Up

Description:
AI chatbots often guess confidently instead of saying "I don't know", and a 2025 paper by researchers at OpenAI and Georgia Tech explains why. This short animated explainer shows the two reasons, using a birthday question and a multiple-choice exam, and the simple scoring change the researchers propose.

What is covered:
- The birthday test: three tries, three different wrong dates
- What a "hallucination" is: an answer that sounds right but is false
- Why facts seen only once in training are hard (if 1 in 5 birthdays appears once, expect at least 1 in 5 wrong)
- Quick question: on an exam, should you leave it blank or guess?
- Why most popular AI tests reward guessing: "I don't know" scores zero
- The fix: make wrong answers cost points (answer only if more than 75% sure, a mistake costs 3 points)
- What to check when you use a chatbot: names, dates, numbers and quotes

The chatbot, exam sheets and score cards are simplified drawings; the "higher score / lower score" comparison is illustrative.
