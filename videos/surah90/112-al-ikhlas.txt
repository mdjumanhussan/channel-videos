TITLE:
Surah Al-Ikhlas in 90 Seconds: Who Is Allah?

CAPTION:
Who is Allah? Four short verses answer it. 🤍

Surah Al-Ikhlas (112). Al-Ikhlas means sincerity.

1️⃣ "Say, 'He is Allah, [who is] One.'" (112:1)
One. No partner. No second.

2️⃣ "Allah, the Eternal Refuge." (112:2)
Everyone and everything needs Him. He needs no one.

3️⃣ "He neither begets nor is born." (112:3)
No children. No parents. He is the First and the Last.

4️⃣ "Nor is there to Him any equivalent." (112:4)
Nothing and no one is like Him.

🧠 Memorize it: read each verse 3 times, then join them. Recite it in your next prayer.

📖 Source: Quran, Surah Al-Ikhlas 112:1–4 (Saheeh International)
