TITLE:
Surah Al-Masad in 90 Seconds: The Uncle Named in the Quran

CAPTION:
Only one of the Prophet's enemies is named in the Quran. He was his own uncle. 🤍

Surah Al-Masad (111). Al-Masad means palm fibre.

📍 Why it was revealed: the Prophet ﷺ called his people from the hill of Safa and warned them. His uncle Abu Lahab rejected him in front of everyone. Then this surah was revealed. (Sahih al-Bukhari 4971)

1️⃣ "May the hands of Abu Lahab be ruined, and ruined is he." (111:1)

2️⃣ "His wealth will not avail him or that which he gained." (111:2)
Money and status could not save him.

3️⃣ "He will [enter to] burn in a Fire of [blazing] flame." (111:3)
Lahab means flame.

4️⃣ "And his wife [as well] - the carrier of firewood." (111:4)

5️⃣ "Around her neck is a rope of [twisted] fiber." (111:5)

Abu Lahab lived for years after this surah. He never believed.

💡 Wealth and family cannot save anyone. Only faith and good deeds can.

📖 Sources: Quran, Surah Al-Masad 111:1–5 (Saheeh International); Sahih al-Bukhari 4971
