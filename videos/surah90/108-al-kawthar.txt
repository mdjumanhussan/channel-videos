TITLE:
Surah Al-Kawthar in 90 Seconds: They Called Him "Cut Off"

CAPTION:
His enemies said his name would be forgotten. 💔
Allah answered with the shortest surah in the Quran. ✨

"Indeed, We have granted you al-Kawthar. So pray to your Lord and sacrifice [to Him alone]. Indeed, your enemy is the one cut off." (Quran 108:1–3)

🌊 What is al-Kawthar? The Prophet ﷺ said it is a river his Lord promised him, a pond his people will come to on the Day of Resurrection, with cups as many as the stars. (Sahih Muslim 400)
Ibn Abbas said it is all the good Allah gave him, and the river is part of it. (Sahih al-Bukhari 4966)

📢 They called him "cut off". Today his name is called in the adhan, every day, all over the world. ﷺ

🧠 Memorize it today: 3 verses. Read each one 3 times, then join them.

People may mock. Allah gives. 🤍

📖 Sources: Quran 108:1–3 (Saheeh International); Sahih Muslim 400; Sahih al-Bukhari 4966; Tafsir Ibn Kathir on Surah 108
