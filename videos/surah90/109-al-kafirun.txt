Surah Al-Kafirun in 90 Seconds: The Deal the Prophet ﷺ Refused

CAPTION:
They offered him a deal: "One year our gods, one year yours." 🤚
Allah answered with six verses. ✨

Surah Al-Kafirun (109) — 6 verses, revealed in Makkah:
"Say, 'O disbelievers, I do not worship what you worship. Nor are you worshippers of what I worship. Nor will I be a worshipper of what you worship. Nor will you be worshippers of what I worship. For you is your religion, and for me is my religion.'" (Quran 109:1–6)

📌 Why it repeats: verses 2–3 speak of now, verses 4–5 of the future. Not this year. Not next year. Not ever.

🌅 Sunnah: the Prophet ﷺ recited Al-Kafirun and Al-Ikhlas in the two rak'ahs before Fajr. (Sahih Muslim 726)

🧠 Memorize it today: read each verse 3 times, then join them. Recite it before Fajr tomorrow.
