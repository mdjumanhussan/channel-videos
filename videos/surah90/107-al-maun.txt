TITLE:
Surah Al-Ma'un in 90 Seconds: Woe to Those Who Pray?

CAPTION:
"So woe to those who pray." 😳 (Quran 107:4)
Why would Allah warn people who pray? The answer is in the next verses. 👇

Surah Al-Ma'un (107) — 7 verses, revealed in Makkah:
🚪 He drives away the orphan (107:2)
🍲 He does not encourage feeding the poor (107:3)
🕌 He is heedless of his prayer (107:5). Ibn Abbas: they pray in public, but not in private.
🎭 He prays to be seen (107:6)
🪣 He withholds simple help (107:7). Ibn Mas'ud: like an axe, a pot or a bucket you lend a neighbour.

💡 Notice: "heedless OF their prayer", not "in" it. A wandering mind is not what this verse means.

Faith is not only on the prayer mat. Pray for Allah. Be kind to people, even with a pot. 🤍

📖 Sources: Quran 107:1–7 (Saheeh International); Tafsir Ibn Kathir on Surah 107
