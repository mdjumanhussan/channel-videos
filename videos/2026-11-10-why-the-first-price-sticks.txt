Title: Why the First Price You See Sticks
Category: Education

Description:
In a famous 1974 study, a rigged wheel of fortune moved people's guesses by 20 points. This short animated explainer shows the anchoring effect: why the first number you see pulls your judgement, where you meet it every day, and one simple way to protect your wallet.

What is covered:
- The 1974 wheel of fortune experiment (Tversky and Kahneman)
- Anchoring and adjustment: why you stop too soon
- Quick test: 8 x 7 x 6 x 5 x 4 x 3 x 2 x 1 in five seconds
- Estate agents and listing prices, and legal experts and random numbers
- "Was" prices, menus and opening offers: about 37 cents per extra dollar in negotiations
- The limits: which anchoring results hold up and which have mixed repeats
- How to give yourself a second starting point

Prices on the tags and menu are examples, and the adjustment drawings are illustrative.
