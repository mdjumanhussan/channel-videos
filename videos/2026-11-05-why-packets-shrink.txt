LONG VIDEO (post 18:00 Sydney)
Title: Same Price. Smaller Packet.

Description:
The price tag didn't move, but you still paid more. This short animated explainer shows the sum behind shrinkflation, why shoppers react to price far more than to size, and the small label that beats it.

What is covered:
- The ice cream sum: same $5.99, 6.7% more per ounce
- Quick test: which change would you spot?
- What a 2004 Harvard study of snack buyers found
- A 2025 US government review: 2 to 5 times more responsive to a price rise
- Why a smaller pack is harder to catch than a new price
- The twist: official inflation figures count it, and it is rarer than it feels
- Why shoppers call it unfair when they do notice
- The fix: read the unit price on the shelf tag

The ice cream and coffee numbers are worked examples. The shelf tag prices and the two response bars are illustrative. The drawings are simplified for teaching.
