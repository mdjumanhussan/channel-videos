Title:
Why Airlines Sell More Tickets Than There Are Seats

Description:
Airlines often sell more tickets than a plane has seats, and they do it on purpose. This short animated explainer shows why, how they predict who won't turn up, and what happens when everyone does.

What is covered:
- Why an empty seat earns an airline nothing, and why most no-shows are simply running late
- The party trick you already use: more invitations than chairs
- How airlines forecast no-shows from records of past flights
- The law of large numbers: one passenger is unpredictable, a full plane is not
- Quick question: which flight gets overbooked more?
- Volunteers first, and what happens if nobody volunteers
- How rare it is: 0.27 involuntary bumps per 10,000 passengers at the 10 US reporting airlines in early 2024

The plane, ticket numbers and no-show chart are illustrative. This is general information, not legal advice; passenger rights differ by country and airline.
