Title: Mixed Practice Feels Worse. It Works Better.
Category: Education

Description:
In one study, students who did worse during practice scored about three times higher on the test a week later. This short animated explainer shows what mixed (interleaved) practice is, the experiments behind it, and when it helps.

What is covered:
- Blocked practice: one kind of problem at a time
- The 2007 shapes experiment: 89% vs 60% in practice, 63% vs 20% on the test a week later
- Why the block group picked the wrong formula
- The hidden skill: deciding which kind of problem it is
- Quick question: learning painters by their style
- Mixed study: 59% vs 36% on new paintings, though most people judged one-at-a-time better
- The limits: what a review of 59 studies found
- How to mix your own practice

The shapes, paintings and people are drawn for teaching; the people icons are illustrative.
