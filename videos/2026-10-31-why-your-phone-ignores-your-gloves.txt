=== LONG VIDEO ===
Title:
Why Your Phone Ignores Your Gloves

Description:
Tap your phone with gloves on and nothing happens; take one off and it works straight away. This short animated explainer shows how a phone screen feels the electricity in your finger, and why gloves block it.

What is covered:
- The grid of see-through wires under the glass, in rows and columns
- The small electric field at every crossing, and how the phone keeps checking them
- How your fingertip weakens the field at one spot, and how two fingers make pinch to zoom work
- Why wool, leather and cotton are insulators, so nothing changes through a glove
- Quick question: a plastic pen, or a glove with silver thread in the fingertip?
- Touchscreen gloves, phone styluses, and the 2010 reports of sausage styluses in South Korea
- Older pressure screens, like some cash machines and self-checkouts, that do work with gloves

The grid, field dots and readings are simplified for teaching.

Title:
Why don't phones work with gloves? #Shorts

Description:
Your phone's screen senses the electricity in your fingertip, and ordinary gloves don't conduct, so the screen can't feel you through them.
