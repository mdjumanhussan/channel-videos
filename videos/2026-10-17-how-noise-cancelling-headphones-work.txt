Title: How Noise-Cancelling Headphones Work
Audience: No, it's not made for kids. Category: Science & Technology. Visibility: Public.
Tags: how noise cancelling headphones work, active noise cancellation, anc explained, destructive interference, science explained
Description:
Noise-cancelling headphones make things quieter by adding sound: they play the exact opposite of the noise. This short animated explainer shows how that works, and why it beats an engine hum but not a crying baby.

What is covered:
- Sound as a wave of squeezed and spread-out air
- The flip: an opposite wave that cancels the noise (destructive interference)
- Inside the earcup: outside mic, chip, speaker, and the inside mic that checks what is left
- Quick question: engine hum or a baby crying, which is easier to cancel?
- Why steady low sounds are easy and quick high sounds are hard
- The soft seal that blocks high sounds
- An old idea: Paul Lueg's 1930s patent and Amar Bose's 1978 flight

The wave drawings are simplified for teaching.
