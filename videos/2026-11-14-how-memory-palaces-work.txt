Title:
Memory Champions Aren't Smarter. Try Their Trick

Description:
Memory champions can learn about a hundred words in five minutes, yet brain scans suggest their brains are built much like yours. This short animated explainer shows the ancient memory palace trick they use, lets you try it on a shopping list as you watch, and looks at a 2017 study where ordinary people more than doubled their recall in 40 days.

What is covered:
- What brain scans of memory champions found (2003): not smarter, same structure, more use of areas for places and routes
- The legend of Simonides and the collapsed banquet hall
- How the memory palace (method of loci) works: a place you know, stops in order, vivid pictures
- Try it: five things to buy, placed around your home
- Why it works: every picture gets an address, and the route keeps the order
- The 2017 study: 26 words to 62 after 40 days of 30-minute practice
- The catch: it takes practice

The house, icons, scans and connection patterns are simplified drawings; the legend scene is drawn for illustration.
