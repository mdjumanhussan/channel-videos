Title: Why Your Voice Sounds Wrong on Recordings

Description:
Most people cringe when they hear their own voice on a recording. This short animated explainer shows the two roads your voice takes to your inner ear, why the road through your head makes you sound deeper to yourself, and what a 2013 study found when people rated their own voice without knowing it.

What is covered:
- Air conduction and bone conduction: two roads to the inner ear
- Why your head boosts the deep, low parts of your voice
- Try it: fingers in your ears, then hum
- Why a microphone makes you sound higher and thinner
- Which voice other people hear
- Voice confrontation: why it feels so bad
- The 2013 study: 80 people, their own voice hidden in the mix

The head drawings are simplified, and the bars and star scores are illustrative.
