Title: Caffeine Doesn't Give You Energy. It Hides Tiredness
Audience: No, it's not made for kids. Category: Education. Visibility: Public.
Tags: how caffeine works, does coffee give you energy, adenosine, caffeine and sleep, science explained
Description:
Caffeine adds no energy to your body. It blocks the chemical signal that tells your brain you are tired, and this short animated explainer shows how, how long it lasts, and why the tiredness comes back.

What is covered:
- The dashboard idea: caffeine is tape over a fuel warning light
- Adenosine, the tiredness chemical that builds up the longer you are awake
- How caffeine sits in adenosine's receptors without switching them on
- How long it lasts: starts working in about 30 minutes; half-life about 5 hours in an average adult (2 to 12 hours, varies)
- Quick question: a 2013 study of caffeine taken 6 hours before bed
- Why the tiredness is still there when caffeine wears off
- Tolerance, withdrawal headaches, and the FDA's 400 mg a day figure for most healthy adults

The drawings and the caffeine curve are simplified for teaching. This is general information, not medical advice.
