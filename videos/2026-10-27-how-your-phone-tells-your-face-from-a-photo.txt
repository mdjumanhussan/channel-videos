Title:
How Your Phone Tells Your Face From a Photo

Description:
In a 2023 test, a printed photo unlocked 19 of 48 phones, while others could not be fooled that way at all. This short animated explainer shows the difference: some face unlock only looks at your face, and some measures it with invisible dots.

What is covered:
- Simple face unlock: comparing two flat pictures, and why a photo can pass
- 3D face unlock (e.g. Face ID): infrared light that works in the dark
- More than 30,000 invisible dots, and why they shift over a bumpy surface
- The depth map: the 3D shape of your face
- Quick question: what does the depth map show for a life-size photo?
- The match: a code of numbers, and Apple's "less than 1 in 1,000,000" figure
- Limits: twins, lookalike siblings, children under 13; attention check; beards and makeup
- Where the face data is kept, and a safer lock if your phone uses the camera only

The dots, depth map and code numbers are simplified for teaching.
