Title: Your Brain Can't Really Multitask (It Switches)
Audience: No, it's not made for kids. Category: Education. Visibility: Public.
Tags: multitasking, can your brain multitask, task switching, focus, psychology explained
Description:
When you think you are doing two thinking tasks at once, your brain is really switching between them, and every switch costs time. This short animated explainer shows what happens in each switch and what the research found.

What is covered:
- A quick try-it test: counting and letters, separately and mixed
- Earl Miller (MIT): we switch between tasks very rapidly
- The two hidden steps in every switch: goal shifting and rule activation
- A 2001 study: complex and unfamiliar tasks cost the most time
- Quick question: laundry and TV, or an email and a phone call?
- A 2015 study: even an unanswered call or text led to more mistakes
- Supertaskers: about 1 in 40 people in a 2010 driving-simulator study

The bar charts and time blocks are simplified for teaching.
