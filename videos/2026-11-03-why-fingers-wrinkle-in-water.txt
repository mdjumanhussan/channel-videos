LONG VIDEO (post 18:00 Sydney)
Title: Your Nerves Wrinkle Your Fingers

Description:
Bath wrinkles look like soggy skin, but a wrinkled finger is actually smaller, not bigger. This short animated explainer shows how your nerves narrow the blood vessels under your fingertips so the skin buckles into grooves, and why scientists still disagree about the reason.

What is covered:
- Why the swollen-skin explanation falls short
- The 1930s clue: fingers with damaged nerves stay smooth in water
- How narrowed blood vessels make the fingertip shrink and the skin buckle
- The rain-tread idea, and why grooves look like rivers
- Quick question: did wrinkled fingers move wet marbles faster?
- The 2013 result, and the 2014 test that found no advantage
- A small 2025 study: wrinkles come back in almost the same pattern

The fingertip, skin and blood vessel drawings are simplified for teaching, and are not to scale. This video is general science, not medical advice.
