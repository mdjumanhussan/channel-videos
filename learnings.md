# Learnings

What we learn from high-performing educational videos and from this channel's own numbers.
Each run adds 2–4 lessons and applies one. Lessons are patterns, never copied material.

## Starting points (already built into the engine, not yet tested against real numbers)

- Content: open with a question or a surprising true statement in the first 5 seconds. One idea per scene. End with a 3-point recap.
- Visuals: one bright focal element per scene on a dark background; slow camera push-in; quick settle on each cut.
- Graphic design: thumbnail with 2–3 short upper-case lines, last line in yellow, one simple picture on the right.
- Sound: narration first; quiet music bed about 13 dB under the voice; soft whoosh on scene changes; a deep hit on the main reveal.
