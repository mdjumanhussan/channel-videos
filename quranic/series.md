# Quranic Story — series plan

Order: all Quranic stories first (roughly chronological), then hadith stories.
One episode per day. The daily job takes the first row with an empty "Built" column, then fills it in.
Verse references are starting points. The daily job confirms every quoted verse against `data/quran.json` (the engine does this automatically for verse cards).
